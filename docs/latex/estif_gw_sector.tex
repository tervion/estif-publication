\section{Gravitational-Wave Sector: $c_\text{gw} = c$ Derived}
\label{sec:gwsector}

Earlier versions of this framework obtained a luminal wave speed for the flow
field only under an explicit hypothesis (``Assumption A'': a relativistic locking
$\mu = \kappa/c^2$ of inertia to stiffness). That assumption is no longer needed.
Under the amended axioms the propagation speed of gravitational waves is a
\emph{derived} quantity, with no free parameter.

\subsection{Setup}

Axiom A1$'$ states that the 3D slices are even on average, with matter sourcing
local deviations; A2 states that everything moves through the 4D bulk at the
universal speed $c$. From these, the derivation of the field equation
(Gauss--Codazzi / ADM in Painlev\'e--Gullstrand gauge) produces a \emph{single
Lorentzian geometry}: the Lorentzian signature is an output of the construction,
not an input. Axiom A3 (vacuum sources nothing), together with the Phase-2
result that the residual stress sector permitted by the axioms is empty, forbids
any second metric, field, or medium on which waves could propagate at a
different speed.

\subsection{Statement and derivation}

Transverse-traceless perturbations are ripples \emph{of} that one geometry, and
light propagates \emph{on} its null cones. The principal symbol of the vacuum TT
wave operator is $g^{\mu\nu}k_\mu k_\nu$ --- the same null cone that governs
photon propagation. This is shown symbolically for an \emph{arbitrary} flow
profile: the radial null speed is $u = v \pm c$, identical for gravitational
waves and for light, for any $v(r,t)$. Hence

\begin{center}
\fbox{\ \ \parbox{0.85\textwidth}{\centering
\textbf{Result (C-15, derived).}\\[2pt]
$\displaystyle c_\text{gw} = c$ \quad exactly, for arbitrary flow, with no free
parameter.}\ \ }
\end{center}

\noindent
No dial exists in the framework that could produce $c_\text{gw} \neq c$: the
structure that would be required (a second metric or an independent propagation
medium) is exactly what A3 plus the empty residual sector forbid.

\subsection{Confrontation with observation}

The multimessenger bound from GW170817/GRB\,170817A,
$|c_\text{gw} - c|/c \lesssim 10^{-15}$ \cite{LIGO2017GW170817}, is therefore
passed \emph{structurally}: the predicted deviation is exactly zero. This is a
stronger statement than consistency within errors --- the framework cannot
produce a violation of this bound at any parameter values.

\subsection{Relation to conditional strong-field figures}

Because the derived ESTIF vacuum is exactly Schwarzschild and $c_\text{gw} = c$
holds in vacuum, any \emph{deviation} from General Relativity in vacuum
gravitational-wave propagation --- including the LISA Shapiro-type delay figure
quoted elsewhere in this document --- cannot arise from the vacuum sector. Such
figures are conditional on the un-derived non-vacuum sector (limitation C1). The
zero-deviation result of this section is unconditional.
