## 1. What Gaztañaga actually claims (peer-reviewed, 2019–2023)

Gaztañaga's causal-boundary programme fixes the cosmological constant Λ — a free parameter in
Einstein's equations — with a boundary condition, rather than treating it as dark energy or
modified gravity. Key papers:

- **Gaztañaga 2019, "The cosmological constant and the size of the Causal Universe"**
  (arXiv:1904.08218). Boundary condition: test particles should be free when outside causal
  contact. Consequences: a constant vacuum energy does *not* drive cosmic expansion; acceleration
  requires either a large universe with evolving DE (w > −1), *or* a **finite causal boundary
  without DE**. The finite Causal Universe **naturally explains Ω_Λ ≃ 2.3 Ωm today**, which
  evolving DE cannot. Combined with the observed CMB lack of correlations above 60°, the boundary
  condition **predicts Ω_Λ ≈ 0.70 for a flat universe, independent of any other measurement**
  (hence Ωm ≈ 0.30).
- **Gaztañaga 2019, "Homogeneity and the causal boundary"** (arXiv:1911.13199). A finite-age
  universe has a finite causal scale χ_S; the metric cannot be homogeneous beyond it. The causal
  boundary condition fixes Λ; χ_S depends on the details of inflation.
- **Gaztañaga 2021, "The Cosmological Constant as a Zero Action Boundary"** (arXiv:2101.07368).
  Sharpest mechanism: the on-shell gravitational action is zero for a perfect-fluid solution
  ("zero action principle"), which fixes Λ to the average density and pressure inside the
  primordial (inflationary) causal boundary: **Λ = 4πG⟨ρ + 3p⟩**. Other contributions to Λ
  (DE, MG) are renormalized away by the boundary and do not produce expansion.
- **Gaztañaga 2022, "How the Big Bang Ends up Inside a Black Hole"** (Universe 8, 257,
  arXiv:2204.11608) and **"The Black Hole Universe, Part I/II"** (Symmetry 14, 1849 / 1984);
  **"The mass of our observable Universe"** (MNRAS 521, L59, 2023). Later reframing: cosmic
  expansion originates from gravitational collapse and bounce, placing us inside a black hole
  whose horizon is the causal boundary — presented as removing the need for inflation, dark
  matter, *and* dark energy.

**Summary of the prior art:** a causal / particle-horizon boundary condition ties Λ to the
matter content, yields Ω_Λ ≈ 0.70 (Ωm ≈ 0.30) with no dark-energy substance, and explains the
Ω_Λ ≈ 2.3 Ωm coincidence. Published, peer-reviewed, 2019–2023.

## 4. Are they the same result in different notation?

**Substantially yes on the concept; not identical on the mechanism.**

- **Shared core (Gaztañaga's, and prior):** both are causal/particle-horizon boundary
  conditions; both tie the dark sector (Λ ↔ Ωm) to the horizon; both yield Ωm ≈ 0.30 *without
  dark energy as a substance*; both are, at heart, statements about gravity/acceleration at the
  causal boundary. This is enough overlap that the bootstrap must be regarded as a **rediscovery
  of Gaztañaga's published result via a different route**, not an independent new idea.
- **Genuine differences:** the *conditions* differ — Gaztañaga imposes *free* test particles
  (zero action; the net acceleration/action vanishes at the boundary), whereas ESTIF sets the
  matter pull to the specific nonzero value cH₀/2. The boundaries differ (inflationary χ_S vs
  post-inflation r_p). The numbers are close but not identical (≈0.30 vs 0.3043/0.31408). And
  the derivations are formally distinct (a variational zero-action principle vs a geometric
  horizon ratio / force balance).

So: **same neighbourhood, same headline number, same elimination of DE-as-substance — different
mechanism in the details.** The "different mechanism" is real but is *not* enough to claim
novelty on the *result*, because the result (horizon boundary → Ωm ≈ 0.3, no DE) is what
Gaztañaga published.

## 5. What, if anything, is original to ESTIF

Two candidates, honestly weighted:

1. **The a₀ / MOND connection (the stronger claim).** ESTIF's boundary condition is *the same
   cH₀ that sets the MOND acceleration scale* a₀ = H₀cx₀/√3. Gaztañaga's programme does not
   touch galactic dynamics or a₀ at all. If the identity "mean pull at horizon = cH₀/2 = the
   MOND scale" is a mechanism rather than a coincidence, that link is genuinely absent from the
   prior art and is ESTIF's best shot at an original contribution. **But** it is currently
   unexplained (is it mechanism or coincidence? — undetermined), and a₀'s empirical target
   carries ~10% scatter, so this is a lead, not yet a result.
2. **The mechanism (the weaker claim).** Deriving the boundary from a 4D inward-flow picture,
   rather than a zero-action variational principle, is a different *route*. On its own this is
   not novelty — reaching a published number by a new derivation is worthwhile but is not a new
   result, and in ESTIF's case principle P is *not even derived* from the flow axioms (Part B,
   open). Until P is derived from A1–A3, ESTIF has *less* first-principles support for the shared
   result than Gaztañaga does, not more.

## 6. Verdict and required actions

**Verdict:** 🔴 **No novelty claim on Ωm is defensible.** Gaztañaga has priority (2019+,
peer-reviewed) on the causal-horizon → Ωm ≈ 0.3 / no-DE result. Presenting the ESTIF bootstrap
as a novel derivation of Ωm would be caught immediately and would damage the framework's
credibility. The honest framing is: *ESTIF's horizon relation reproduces, by a flow-based route,
the causal-boundary result of Gaztañaga (2019–2023); its potentially distinctive feature is the
identification of that horizon scale with the MOND acceleration a₀, which the prior work does
not address.*

**Required before any Ωm text ships:**
1. **Cite Gaztañaga prominently** (at minimum 1904.08218, 2101.07368; note the black-hole-universe
   series 2204.11608 etc.) wherever principle P, the bootstrap, or Ωm = R_H/r_p appears.
2. **Downgrade the language everywhere** from "prediction/derivation of Ωm" to "reproduces the
   causal-boundary result; consistent with Gaztañaga." The C2 consistency-relation status stands.
3. **Reframe the contribution** around the a₀ link, not the Ωm value — and flag that link as an
   open question (mechanism vs coincidence), not an established result.
4. **Check the numbers against Gaztañaga's** directly: does 0.3043/0.31408 agree with his Ω_Λ ≈
   0.70 within stated uncertainties, and does his Λ = 4πG⟨ρ+3p⟩ reduce, in any limit, to the
   cH₀/2 pull condition? If it does reduce, they are the *same* condition and even the "different
   mechanism" claim weakens further. If it does not, document precisely where they diverge — that
   divergence is the only place original content could live.
